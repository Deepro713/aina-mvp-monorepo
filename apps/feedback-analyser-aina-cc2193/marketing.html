FAQ

What does this actually do?

You paste customer comments, one per line, and your connected model labels each with a sentiment, a category and a summary of 25 words or fewer. Every entry is saved, and a dashboard counts them by sentiment and category.

Who is this for, and who isn't it for?

It's for a PM or founder at a small software company who sorts feedback by hand and already has a model API key. It's not for teams that need shared dashboards, Zendesk or Intercom sync, custom categories, or handling of regulated data.

Why not just use ChatGPT and a spreadsheet?

ChatGPT's labels drift between sessions, and nothing is saved in one place. Here the 7 categories never change, every entry is stored with its date, and the counts are already done.

What does it cost? Is there a contract?

V1 has no billing and no contract. Your only cost is what your model provider charges per call, which is capped by 25 entries per batch and 500 analyses a day.
